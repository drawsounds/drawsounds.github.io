# Draw Sounds

> *Draw sounds came from a shower thought — what if Kid Pix was a sound editor?*

**[Live Demo](https://drawsounds.github.io)**

Draw Sounds is a touch-first musical sketchpad where drawing is composing. Pick a sound color, scribble a melodic line, stamp a rhythmic beat, flood-fill a shape with solid harmony, and press play to hear your picture come to life as a musical score.
